Changelog

Updated 29 October 2026.

  • 29 Oct 2026
    Lost sheep

    A pin that does not count is now called a Lost sheep (miss), after the parable in Luke 15. It still travels 0 km.

  • 29 Oct 2026
    Question fixes after an audit

    Thirty-five questions got corrected answers or facts. Palestine now counts on sixteen Bible questions where a West Bank or Gaza place fits, and Lesotho, the United States (Hawaii), American Samoa, Timor-Leste, the United Kingdom, Colombia, Kosovo, Israel, Belize, Peru, Turkey and the Vatican were added where each fits. A few wrong dates and counts were fixed. Jordan and Lebanon no longer count for the Josiah question, American Samoa no longer counts for the 2019-20 temple announcements, and Australia no longer counts for the Russell M. Nelson question. Three questions on day 3 were reworded to be clearer; their answers did not change. Source links were added to eleven questions after each fact was checked against its page, the Antioch fact on the Bible capitals question now cites Acts correctly, and the Gaza fact on the Acts question was reworded. Days and question order did not move.

  • 28 Oct 2026
    Easier phone pins

    The whole map fits on a phone. Close taps zoom in so you can choose the place with a second tap. You can play any open day with these controls.

  • 26 Oct 2026
    Guide and info pages

    A full guide to play, plus About, Blog, Rulings and this changelog. The game also fits a phone screen.

  • 26 Oct 2026
    Clearer prompts

    Every prompt now fits on one line and starts with "Pin a country". Sources moved to the answer card.

  • 26 Oct 2026
    Share, sound and the names-off bonus

    Share your day as two lines of text. Sound effects can be turned off. With names off, each correct pin travels 10% further.

  • 26 Oct 2026
    Logbook, passport and souvenirs

    Look back at past days, collect countries at every tier, and bring souvenirs home from camp. Past week keeps seven days open.

  • 26 Oct 2026
    First expedition

    Five prompts a day, a map to pin, and the trail from Nauvoo to the Salt Lake Valley.
